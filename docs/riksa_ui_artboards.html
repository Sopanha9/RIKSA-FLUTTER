Understanding Slope & Intercept

យល់ដឹងអំពីមេគុណប្រាប់ទិស

Figure 1.1: Linear Graph with positive slope m = 2

💡 Key Concept Formula
y = mx + b

m represents the steepness (rise over run), and b represents the y-intercept.

Worked Example:

Find the slope of 2y - 4x = 6.
1. Add 4x to both sides: 2y = 4x + 6
2. Divide everything by 2: y = 2x + 3
Result: Slope (m) = 2, Intercept (b) = 3.

Why do we move this number to the other side in: 2x + 5 = 13?

Great question! We don’t actually just “move” it—we apply the same operation to both sides to keep the equation balanced.

Step 1: Subtract 5 from both sides
2x + 5 - 5 = 13 - 5
Step 2: Simplify
2x = 8
Step 3: Divide both sides by 2
x = 4

How to prove that sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 using unit circle geometry?

I need to prepare for BacII exam next month. Can anyone provide an intuitive explanation?

✓ Verified Peer Solution (14 Total Answers)

Vanna Rath

Accepted

On a unit circle of radius r = 1, any point on the circumference is (cos θ, sin θ). By the Pythagorean theorem: x² + y² = r², therefore cos²θ + sin²θ = 1².
